Who We Are

Our Mission

Business Investment Advisory (BIA) is a transaction-focused infrastructure advisory firm specializing in project structuring, credit enhancement, and capital mobilization across emerging markets.

Operating as a development finance intermediary, BIA plays a critical role in bridging projects and capital, enabling efficient execution and successful financial close across key sectors.
