About BIA

Bridging Projects & Capital

A transaction-focused infrastructure advisory firm enabling efficient execution and successful financial close across key sectors in emerging markets.

Our Mission

Transforming Vision into Sustainable Infrastructure

Business Investment Advisory (BIA) is a transaction-focused infrastructure advisory firm specializing in project structuring, credit enhancement, and capital mobilization across emerging markets.

Operating as a development finance intermediary, BIA plays a critical role in bridging projects and capital, enabling efficient execution and successful financial close across key sectors.

Our leadership is focused on transforming strategic opportunities into investment-ready projects — creating employment, strengthening industries and generating lasting economic value.

Our Approach

Structuring

Developing bankable frameworks that attract investment and ensure project viability.

Partnership

Collaborating with governments, developers, and institutional investors.

Execution

Driving transactions from origination through successful financial close.

Chief Executive Officer

Roy Patrick Miranda

International EPC-F & Infrastructure Executive

Roy Patrick Miranda is an international executive with decades of leadership experience spanning engineering, manufacturing, infrastructure, hospitality, investment facilitation and EPC/EPC-F project development across Africa and international markets.

His career combines strong hands-on engineering and operational expertise with senior executive leadership, providing a practical understanding of how complex projects progress from concept development and feasibility through engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, financing coordination and operational delivery.

A significant part of his professional career has been within the Coca-Cola system in Africa, where he held senior technical, operational, design and project-management responsibilities across several countries — including greenfield developments, plant expansions, engineering upgrades and international project management. His international experience spans the United Kingdom, Kenya, Somalia, Sudan, Eritrea, Ghana, Rwanda, Tanzania, Djibouti, Liberia and Nigeria.

As CEO of BIA Limited, Roy focuses on developing strategic partnerships that bring together project owners, governments, EPC contractors, financial institutions and international investors to advance commercially viable and sustainable projects.
