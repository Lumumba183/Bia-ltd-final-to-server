Our Sectors

Where We Create Impact

Strategic advisory, project development, transaction structuring and capital mobilisation across the sectors that build economies.

01

Renewable Energy & Oil & Gas

We provide strategic advisory, project development, transaction structuring, and capital mobilisation across renewable energy, conventional energy, and oil & gas value chains. Our expertise spans project origination, EPC+F solutions, public-private partnerships (PPPs), and investment facilitation.

02

Agriculture & Carbon Markets

BIA supports the development of commercially viable agricultural projects, climate-smart initiatives, and carbon credit programmes. We structure sustainable investments that enhance food security, environmental stewardship, and long-term value creation.

03

Hospitality

We advise investors, developers, and operators on hospitality projects, offering market assessments, investment structuring, feasibility studies, project financing, and strategic partnerships to deliver world-class tourism and hospitality assets.

04

Healthcare

Our healthcare advisory services focus on the planning, financing, and implementation of hospitals, medical facilities, pharmaceutical infrastructure, and healthcare technology projects that improve access to quality healthcare services.

05

Infrastructure Advisory & Bankable Feasibility Studies

BIA delivers end-to-end infrastructure advisory services, including project identification, technical and financial feasibility studies, transaction advisory, investment structuring, PPP advisory, and capital mobilisation. We transform concepts into bankable, investment-ready projects across transport, telecommunications, water, sanitation, energy, housing, and industrial infrastructure.
